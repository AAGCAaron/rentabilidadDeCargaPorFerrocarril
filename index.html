Madrid Forum • Keynote Legal Framework & Technical Aspects

Freight rail differences between North America – Europe

Legal Framework

North America (U.S. & Mexico) Private Sector
  • • Vertical integration: Infrastructure and operation are integrated
  • • Market: Exclusive territory with interline exchanges
  • • Dominance: Freight rail
  • • Main investor: Private sector
Europe (EU & Spain) Public / Mixed
  • • Vertical separation: Infrastructure management separated from operation
  • • Market: Open access
  • • Dominance: Passenger trains
  • • Main investor: Public sector, evolving towards a public-private mix
Takeaway: Very different roles for the private and public sectors.

Technical Aspects Comparison

Metric Spain EU Mexico United States
Length of haul (km) 350 250 600 800
Length of sidings (mts) 750 750 2,500 3,000
Gross weight/car (ton) 90 90 130 130
Rail modal share vs truck (%) 4% 17% 25% 30%
Convoy Capacity & Siding Length 4x Longer in North America
Takeaway: Heavier and longer trains over larger distances compete better with trucks.
Madrid Forum • Keynote Multimodal Dynamics

Competition and/or collaboration with trucks?

Truck Size and Weight Legislation

Truck size and weight legislation is very important. Heavier and longer trucks make uneven competition for rail.

Intermodal Evolution

Evolution from trucking companies now offering intermodal services for long haul.

Maritime Container Companies

Very strong partners for investing in terminals and operating block trains (single origin and destiny for one client).

Takeaways: Shippers need both rail and trucks. Intermodal is on the rise.

Madrid Forum • Keynote PSR & Profitability Metric

Operational efficiency

PSR

Precision Scheduled Railroading

Precision Scheduled Railroading (PSR) is a proven method for optimization of rail operations, but within acceptable limits for shippers and unions.

⚠️ Balancing network efficiency with customer service reliability and labor agreements.

Continuous Improvement

Continuous improvement is needed (processes, regulation, technology, market innovation, human resources realignment…) for profitability.

Key Variable for Capital Returns
2¢ USD
Per Ton / Mile

2 cents of USD per ton/mile is the key variable for making investments profitable on a 50-year horizon.

Madrid Forum • Keynote Final Remarks

Final remarks

Rule of Law

Rule of law is key for private sector involvement for the long term. Legal certainty and strict adherence to concession contracts are non-negotiable prerequisites.

Government Agency Coordination

Private sector needs to have very good coordination with government agencies. Close alignment with transportation, customs, environmental, and infrastructure authorities.

Sustainable Horizon

Long-term profitability enables sustainable decarbonization, high-capacity modal shift, and economic integration.

Rule of Law Certainty for 50-Year Investments
